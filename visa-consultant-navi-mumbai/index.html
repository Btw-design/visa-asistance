Consulate jurisdiction

Navi Mumbai is in Maharashtra, which falls in the consular district of the US Consulate General Mumbai. You do not need to travel to Delhi for a US visa.

Getting there by train

The Harbour line connects Panvel, Kharghar, Belapur, Nerul and Vashi to Kurla, the nearest major station to Bandra Kurla Complex. From Airoli, take the Trans-Harbour line and change for the Harbour line.

Getting there by road

From Vashi, the Sion-Panvel Highway leads towards Bandra Kurla Complex. For Mahalaxmi and Nariman Point, applicants from Belapur, Kharghar and Panvel can also use the Atal Setu sea bridge into south Mumbai.

Centres move and each country uses its own provider. We confirm the address on your appointment letter before you set out.
