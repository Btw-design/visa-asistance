Authorised visa application centres

These authorized service providers accept visa applications on behalf of foreign embassies and consulates. They are located in most major Indian cities and allow applicants to submit biometric data and documents before the embassy processes their visa.

VFS Global Operates visa application centers for UK, Australia, New Zealand, Canada, and many Schengen countries in 15+ Indian cities. BLS International Processes visa applications for Spain, Portugal, Malta, Czech Republic, and other countries across 30+ cities in India. TLScontact Handles visa applications for France, Netherlands, Belgium, and other Schengen states from centers in New Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Bengaluru, and Hyderabad. FAQ

Questions about embassies

An embassy is the main diplomatic mission located in a country's capital city, headed by an ambassador. A consulate is a smaller diplomatic office usually located in major cities outside the capital, providing limited services like visa processing and citizen assistance. Embassies handle broader diplomatic relations, while consulates focus on specific services.

Yes, most embassies require you to schedule an appointment for visa services, notarial services, or consular assistance. Walk-in services are generally not available for visa applications. Always check the embassy's website for appointment booking procedures before visiting.

Generally, you should apply at the embassy or consulate of the country you plan to visit in your country of residence. Some countries allow you to apply at any embassy worldwide, but most require you to apply in your home country or the country where you have legal residence status.

Processing times vary significantly by country and visa type. Tourist visas can take anywhere from 3-15 business days, while student or work visas may take several weeks or months. Check our country-specific visa guides for detailed processing time information.

Required documents typically include a valid passport (with at least 6 months validity), completed visa application form, passport-sized photographs, travel itinerary, proof of accommodation, bank statements, employment letter, and travel insurance. Check the specific visa requirements page for your destination country on our website.

If your visa is rejected, you may reapply with additional supporting documents or appeal the decision depending on the country's policies.
