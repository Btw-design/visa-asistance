Do Indians need a visa for the United Kingdom?

Indian passport holders need a Standard Visitor visa for the UK. It costs £135, allows stays of up to 6 months, and a decision usually takes 3 weeks. You apply online and give biometrics at a visa application centre.

Documents required

Official sources do not give a full list for every case; these are the documents applicants are normally asked for. Confirm the exact list when you apply.

  • Passport
  • Proof you can support yourself during the trip, such as bank statements and payslips
  • Details of where you will stay and your travel plans
  • Evidence that you will leave the UK at the end of the visit

How to apply

1

Apply online on GOV.UK and pay the fee.

2

Book an appointment at a UK visa application centre in India.

3

Attend to give your fingerprints and photo, and provide your documents.

4

Wait for the decision, usually within 3 weeks.

Processing time

Usually within 3 weeks; you can apply up to 3 months before you travel.
