Do Indians need a visa for Costa Rica?

Indian passport holders need a consular visa for Costa Rica unless they hold certain visas or residence permits, such as a valid multiple-entry US or Canadian visa. Stays for Indian visitors are up to 30 days and can be extended.

When you do not need a visa

You can enter without a visa if you hold one of the following:

  • A valid US or Canadian visa that allows multiple entries
  • A valid residence permit from the United States or Canada that allows multiple entries
  • A residence permit from an EU country, the United Kingdom, Iceland, Norway or Switzerland that allows multiple entries or has at least 90 days of validity

These exemptions come from Costa Rica’s General Visa Guidelines. Confirm that your visa or permit qualifies with a Costa Rican consulate before you travel.

Documents required

The official source lists these documents:

  • Valid, machine-readable passport
  • Visa, if required
  • Proof of economic means: at least US$100 per month or part month of stay
  • Return ticket or proof of onward travel

How to apply

1

Check whether a visa or residence permit you already hold exempts you.

2

If you need a visa, apply in person to a Costa Rican consulate. The consular visa must be requested personally by the applicant.

3

On arrival, show your passport, your return or onward ticket and proof of funds of at least US$100 per month of stay.

4

The immigration officer decides the length of your stay when you enter.
