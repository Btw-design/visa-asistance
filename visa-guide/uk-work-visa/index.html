UK Work Visa for Indians: Skilled Worker, Health and Care, and Young Professionals Routes

Most Indians who work in the UK do so on a Skilled Worker visa, which needs a job offer from a Home Office-approved employer. Health professionals use the Health and Care Worker visa, and 18 to 30 year olds can try the India Young Professionals Scheme ballot.

Overview

The UK has no general work permit. Each route is a visa with its own rules. Sponsored routes need a certificate of sponsorship from an employer licensed by the Home Office; the Young Professionals Scheme does not.

A Standard Visitor visa does not let you take a job in the UK.

The Skilled Worker visa can lead to settlement: GOV.UK states you can apply for indefinite leave to remain after five years if you are eligible.

Work routes

RouteWho it is forJob offer or sponsor needed?
Skilled Worker visaPeople with a job offer in an eligible occupation from an approved employerYes. A certificate of sponsorship is required
Health and Care Worker visaQualified doctors, nurses, health professionals and adult social care professionalsYes. A certificate of sponsorship from an approved health or care employer
India Young Professionals SchemeIndian citizens aged 18 to 30 with an eligible qualification, selected in a ballotNo job offer needed

GOV.UK lists other work visas, such as Global Talent and Graduate, with their own criteria. Check GOV.UK if one of those fits you better.

Eligibility

Skilled Worker visa

  • A confirmed job offer from a UK employer approved by the Home Office.
  • A certificate of sponsorship from that employer.
  • A job on the list of eligible occupations. Higher-skilled occupations are eligible; medium-skilled ones only if they are on the immigration salary list or temporary shortage list.
  • A salary of at least GBP 41,700 a year or the going rate for the job, whichever is higher.
  • A lower threshold of GBP 33,400 in some cases set out by GOV.UK. Healthcare and education jobs follow national pay scales.
  • The ability to speak, read, write and understand English.
  • At least GBP 1,270 in savings, unless your sponsor certifies your maintenance.

Health and Care Worker visa

  • A qualified doctor, nurse, health professional or adult social care professional.
  • An eligible health or social care job with an approved UK employer.
  • A certificate of sponsorship.
  • A minimum salary that depends on the type of work.

India Young Professionals Scheme

  • An Indian citizen aged 18 to 30.
  • An eligible qualification.
  • GBP 2,530 in personal savings.
  • Selection in the scheme ballot, followed by an invitation to apply.

Job offer and sponsorship

For the Skilled Worker and Health and Care Worker visas you must have a confirmed job offer before you apply, and the employer must be approved by the Home Office and assign you a certificate of sponsorship.

The exception is the India Young Professionals Scheme. It does not need a job offer: you enter a ballot and, if selected, can work in most jobs for up to 24 months.

Who does what

Work visa applications fail most often when it is unclear who is responsible for which step. This is how the duties are split.

What the employer does

  • Hold a sponsor licence approved by the Home Office.
  • Assign your certificate of sponsorship.
  • Pay a salary that meets the threshold and the going rate, and UK minimum wage rules.
  • Certify your maintenance on the certificate, if it agrees to cover your first month.

What you do

  • Apply online and prove your identity.
  • Pay the application fee and the healthcare surcharge.
  • Prove your knowledge of English.
  • Show GBP 1,270 in savings held for 28 days, unless exempt.
  • Apply to update your visa before changing employer.

Documents

Common documents

  • Certificate of sponsorship reference number from your employer
  • Valid passport
  • Proof of your knowledge of English
  • Job title, occupation code and annual salary
  • Name of your employer and its sponsor licence number
  • Bank statements showing at least GBP 1,270 held for 28 consecutive days, unless your sponsor covers this

Route-specific documents

How to apply

1

Get a confirmed job offer from a Home Office-approved employer, or enter the Young Professionals ballot.

2

Receive your certificate of sponsorship and check the job, occupation code and salary on it.

3

Apply online on GOV.UK. You can apply up to 3 months before the day you are due to start work.

4

Pay the application fee and the healthcare surcharge.

5

Prove your identity and provide your documents, by app or at a visa application centre.

6

Wait for the decision, usually within 3 weeks when you apply from outside the UK.

7

Travel to the UK and start work on or after the start date on your certificate.

Health and Care Worker visaGBP 324 (up to 3 years); GBP 628 (more than 3 years)Application fee, the same inside or outside the UK

India Young Professionals SchemeGBP 340, plus a healthcare surcharge of GBP 1,552Application fee and surcharge for the 24-month stay

Partners and children pay the same application fee as you for the same length of visa. You must also show GBP 1,270 in savings, or GBP 2,530 for the Young Professionals Scheme.

Processing time

Stage or routeOfficial timeframe
Skilled Worker and Health and Care Worker, applying from outside the UKUsually within 3 weeks
Skilled Worker, applying from inside the UK or updating your visaUsually within 8 weeks
India Young Professionals SchemeUsually within 3 weeks

These are GOV.UK’s usual decision times once you have proved your identity and provided your documents. A faster decision may be available for an extra fee.

Validity and renewal

RouteValidityRenewal and conditions
Skilled Worker visaUp to 5 yearsCan be extended as many times as you like; settlement possible after 5 years
Health and Care Worker visaUp to 5 yearsCan be extended; no healthcare surcharge
India Young Professionals SchemeUp to 24 monthsCannot be extended; you must enter the UK within 6 months of approval

Family and job changes

Dependants

On the Skilled Worker and Health and Care Worker visas, your partner and children may be able to apply to join you as dependants. They pay the same application fee and must show they can support themselves.

On the India Young Professionals Scheme, dependants cannot be included and must apply separately in their own right.

Changing employers

You must apply to update your Skilled Worker visa if you change employer, move to a job in a different occupation code, or leave a job on the immigration salary list for one that is not.

You can apply up to 3 months before the new job starts, but you should not start it until your new permission is confirmed. You can keep working in your current job while you wait.

Common mistakes

  • Accepting an offer from an employer that is not approved by the Home Office.
  • A salary below GBP 41,700 or below the going rate for the occupation code.
  • Savings not held for the full 28 days.
  • Starting a new job before your updated visa is confirmed.
  • Forgetting to budget for the healthcare surcharge for every year of the visa.
  • Assuming a visitor visa lets you work.
