Do Indians need a visa for South Korea?

Indian passport holders need a visa for South Korea. The single-entry tourist visa (C-3-9) costs INR 3,600 for Indians and takes about 2 weeks.

South Korea visa options for Indians

OptionWho it is forStay and validityOfficial fee
Short-term tourist visa (C-3-9), single entryTourismUp to 90 days, as grantedINR 3,600

South Korea visa fees

OptionOfficial fee
Short-term tourist visa (C-3-9), single entryINR 3,600

INR 3,600 is the fee the Korean Embassy in New Delhi lists from 1 August 2026 for a single-entry visa of up to 90 days; a single-entry visa for more than 90 days costs INR 5,400 and a multiple-entry visa INR 8,100. The visa application centre adds a service charge.

Documents required

The official source lists these documents:

  • Passport
  • Visa application form
  • Photo
  • Flight booking
  • Hotel booking
  • Bank statement

How to apply

1

Fill in the visa application form and attach a photo.

2

Collect your flight booking, hotel booking and bank statements.

3

Submit at the Korea visa application centre that covers your state. If you live in Maharashtra, Gujarat, Goa, Madhya Pradesh or Telangana, apply through one of the Mumbai consulate’s designated travel agencies instead: walk-in applications there stopped on 1 October 2026.

4

Collect your passport after about 2 weeks.

Processing time

About 2 weeks.

Visa application centres in India

Korea Visa Application Centres, run by VFS Global, take applications in 4 cities. In the Mumbai consulate’s area you apply through one of its designated travel agencies instead.
