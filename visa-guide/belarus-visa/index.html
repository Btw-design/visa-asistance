Do Indians need a visa for Belarus?

Indian passport holders need a visa for Belarus. Applications are handled by the Embassy of Belarus in New Delhi, by prior appointment only.

The fee is paid in Indian rupees at the Reserve Bank of India exchange rate of the day, through a bank only; the embassy accepts neither cash nor cards. The consular officer confirms the exact rupee amount once the application is accepted.

Documents required

The official source lists these documents:

  • Completed visa application form
  • Passport valid for at least 90 days after your planned departure from Belarus
  • Colour photograph, 35 × 45 mm, taken within the last 6 months
  • Copy of health insurance valid in Belarus, with cover of at least €10,000
  • Hotel booking or proof of rented accommodation
  • Flight itinerary
  • Bank statements for the last 3 months
  • Original visa support documents (such as an invitation)

How to apply

1

Email india.consul@mfa.gov.by to book an appointment; the embassy accepts applications only by prior appointment.

2

Submit your documents at the embassy: 90, Poorvi Marg, Vasant Vihar, New Delhi 110057.

3

Pay the fee through the bank as instructed by the consular officer.

4

Collect your passport after 5 days, or within 48 hours for urgent processing.

Processing time

5 days for standard processing (apply on Monday, collect on Friday morning); within 48 hours for urgent processing.

Best Time to Visit Belarus

Belarus has a temperate continental climate with four distinct seasons. Each season offers different experiences:

  • Summer (June to August): The warmest and most popular season with temperatures of 20-28C. Ideal for exploring Minsk, the Mir Castle, and the Belovezhskaya Pushcha National Park. Outdoor festivals and concerts are common.
  • Spring (April to May): The landscape comes alive with blooming orchards and greenery. Temperatures range from 8-18C. Fewer tourists and lower prices. The snow begins to melt, revealing historical sites.
  • Autumn (September to October): Beautiful fall colors across the countryside. Temperatures of 8-16C. Excellent for photography and cultural tourism. The golden foliage in Nesvizh Park is particularly stunning.
  • Winter (November to March): Cold with temperatures ranging from -5C to -15C. Snow-covered landscapes create a fairytale atmosphere. Perfect for winter sports in Silichi and Logoisk ski resorts. New Year celebrations are a major event.

Travel Tips for Belarus

Discover Belarus with confidence using these practical tips:

  • Currency: Belarusian Ruble (BYN) is the local currency. Credit cards are widely accepted in Minsk's hotels, restaurants, and shops. Cash is essential in smaller towns and rural areas. Exchange currency at official banks only.
  • Transportation: Minsk National Airport (MSQ) is the main international gateway. The Minsk Metro is efficient and affordable. Trains connect Minsk to Brest, Grodno, and Vitebsk. For intercity travel, trains are more reliable than buses.
  • Language: Belarusian and Russian are both official languages. Russian is more commonly spoken in cities. English is limited to hotels, tourist sites, and younger people in Minsk. Learn basic Russian phrases.
  • Local Customs: Belarusians are reserved but warm once you get to know them. Remove shoes when entering homes. Gift-giving is expected when visiting someone's home. Public displays of affection are less common than in Western Europe.
  • Cuisine: Must-try dishes include Draniki (potato pancakes), Borscht (beetroot soup), Kolduny (stuffed potato dumplings), and Machanka (pork stew). Belarusian cuisine is hearty and potato-based. Try local kvass (fermented bread drink).
  • Attractions: Don't miss the Mir Castle Complex (UNESCO), Nesvizh Palace, the Brest Fortress, and Stalin Line museum. Minsk's Independence Avenue is one of Europe's longest streets at 15 km.
