Oman Work Visa for Indians: Labour Permit, Employment Visa and Fees

In Oman the employer does the applying. Your sponsor first gets a labour permit from the Ministry of Labour, then requests a work visa from the Royal Oman Police. The work visa costs OMR 20 and is valid for two years.

Overview

The Royal Oman Police (ROP) grants a work visa at the request, and on the responsibility, of an employer to a foreigner coming to Oman for employment.

Unless the worker is invited by a government organisation, a labour permit from the Ministry of Labour is needed first. The visa must match that permit: same gender and same occupation.

The labour permit and the work visa are separate. One authorises the employer to hire for a role; the other lets you enter and stay to do it.

Eligibility

Work visa

  • You must be at least 21 years old.
  • Your sponsor must be a local sponsor with full legal competence.
  • Your gender must match the one stated in the labour permit.
  • The occupation in the visa application must be the same as in the labour permit.
  • Your passport must be valid for at least 6 months.
  • Indian nationals must provide a medical certificate attested by Oman’s Ministry of Health.
  • Some occupations, such as teaching, medical and media roles, need approval from the relevant organisation.

Job offer and sponsorship

A job offer and a sponsoring employer are essential: the visa is issued on the employer’s responsibility and cannot be requested by the worker.

The only stated exception to the labour permit is a foreigner invited by a government organisation.

Who does what

Work visa applications fail most often when it is unclear who is responsible for which step. This is how the duties are split.

What the employer does

  • Obtain the labour permit from the Ministry of Labour.
  • Apply for the work visa through the Royal Oman Police.
  • Make sure the occupation and details match the labour permit.
  • Renew the visa on time; the ROP lists a monthly fine for delays in renewal.

What you do

  • Provide a passport valid for at least 6 months and photographs.
  • Complete the medical examination and have the certificate attested.
  • Hold any professional approval your occupation needs.
  • For ECR passports: get emigration clearance in India before departure.

Documents

Common documents

  • Electronic visa application
  • Two photographs, 6 x 4 cm
  • Copy of your passport, valid for at least 6 months
  • Labour permit from the Ministry of Labour, with a copy
  • Medical certificate, attested by the Ministry of Health for Indian nationals
  • For ECR passport holders: emigration clearance obtained through the eMigrate system before departure

How to apply

1

Accept a job offer from an employer in Oman.

2

The employer obtains a labour permit for your role from the Ministry of Labour.

3

Complete the medical examination required for Indian nationals and have the certificate attested.

4

The employer submits the work visa application to the Royal Oman Police with your passport copy, photograph, labour permit and medical report.

5

If your passport carries an Emigration Check Required (ECR) endorsement, obtain emigration clearance from the Protector of Emigrants through India’s eMigrate system before you fly. This applies to employment in all 18 ECR countries, which include Oman.

6

Travel to Oman and have the visa stamped. The two-year validity runs from the date of stamping.

Family and job changes

Dependants

Oman has a family joining visa for the family of residents. The Royal Oman Police page did not show its conditions, so confirm the requirements with the ROP before you apply.

Changing employers

The work visa is issued on the responsibility of a named sponsor and must match that sponsor’s labour permit. Changing employer therefore needs a new labour permit and visa arrangement through the new sponsor. Confirm the current procedure with the Ministry of Labour.

Common mistakes

  • Applying when you are under 21.
  • A job title in the visa application that differs from the labour permit.
  • Travelling without the attested medical certificate.
  • A passport with less than 6 months of validity.
  • Leaving India without emigration clearance when your passport has an ECR endorsement.
  • Entering on a tourist visa and working.

FAQs

The Royal Oman Police fee for a work visa is OMR 20.

It is valid for staying in Oman for two years from the date it is stamped in the passport, and it is a multiple-entry visa.

The applicant must be at least 21 years old.

Yes. The Royal Oman Police requires a medical certificate for nationals of India and several other countries, attested by Oman’s Ministry of Health.

The employer. The visa is granted at the request and on the responsibility of the employer, who must first hold a labour permit from the Ministry of Labour.

It is the Ministry of Labour’s authorisation for an employer to hire a foreign worker. The visa’s occupation and gender must match it. It is not needed when a government organisation invites the worker.

Passports without the ECR endorsement do not need it.
