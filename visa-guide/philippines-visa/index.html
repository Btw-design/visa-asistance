Do Indians need a visa for the Philippines?

Indian passport holders can visit the Philippines without a visa for 14 days, for tourism or business. The stay rises to 30 days if you hold a valid visa or residence permit from the USA, Japan, Australia, Canada, a Schengen country, Singapore or the UK.
