New Zealand Work Visa for Indians: Accredited Employer Work Visa and Residence Pathways

The main work visa for New Zealand is the Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV). You need a full-time job offer from an employer accredited by Immigration New Zealand, and the visa ties you to that employer.

Overview

The AEWV is a temporary work visa. Three things have to be in place: the employer is accredited, the job has an approved job check, and you meet the skill requirements.

Residence is a separate step. Skilled residence visas, including the Skilled Migrant Category and the Green List pathways, have their own criteria and fees.

A visitor visa does not let you work in New Zealand.

Immigration New Zealand lists further work visas for specific purposes. This guide covers the routes most Indian employees use.

Eligibility

Accredited Employer Work Visa

  • An offer of full-time work, at least 30 hours a week, from an accredited employer with an approved job check.
  • At least 2 years of relevant work experience, or a level 4 or higher qualification on the New Zealand Qualifications Framework. A bachelor’s degree qualifies in any field.
  • English language evidence if the job is at skill level 3, 4 or 5.
  • Occupational registration, if the job requires it.
  • Good health and character. A chest X-ray is needed for stays of 6 months or more from countries with higher TB rates, and police certificates for stays of 24 months or more.

Skilled Migrant Category Resident Visa

  • Aged 55 or under.
  • One of three pathways: 6 skilled resident points, skilled work experience, or an eligible trades or technician role.
  • From 24 August 2026, the New Zealand work experience required for most migrants is a maximum of 2 years.

Green List pathways

  • Tier 1 roles qualify for the Straight to Residence Visa without New Zealand work experience.
  • Tier 2 roles qualify for the Work to Residence Visa after New Zealand work experience.

Job offer and sponsorship

For the AEWV you need a job offer before you apply, and only an accredited employer can make it. The employer also has to pass a job check for the specific role. The offer must be genuine, available and ongoing, and based in New Zealand.

There is no general job-seeker work visa among these routes.

Who does what

Work visa applications fail most often when it is unclear who is responsible for which step. This is how the duties are split.

What the employer does

  • Hold accreditation with Immigration New Zealand.
  • Get a job check approved for the role.
  • Offer at least 30 hours a week at a wage that meets the requirements.
  • Give you the job details needed for your application.

What you do

  • Show 2 years of relevant experience or a qualifying qualification.
  • Meet the English requirement for skill level 3 to 5 jobs.
  • Provide health and character evidence.
  • Apply online and pay the visa fee.
  • Work only for the accredited employer named on your visa.

Documents

Common documents

  • Valid passport
  • Job offer from your accredited employer, linked to an approved job check
  • Evidence of 2 years of relevant work experience, or your qualification
  • Chest X-ray, if you will stay 6 months or more
  • Police certificates, if you will stay 24 months or more in total
  • Occupational registration, where the job requires it

How to apply

1

Find a job with an employer accredited by Immigration New Zealand.

2

The employer completes the job check and sends you the link to apply.

3

Gather your experience or qualification evidence, and English results if needed.

4

Apply online and pay the fee.

5

Provide your chest X-ray and police certificates when required.

6

Wait for the decision, then travel and start work with that employer.

Family and job changes

Dependants

You cannot include family in an AEWV application. You may be able to support a partner for a visitor or work visa and children for visitor or student visas.

Whether you can do so depends on your income and the skill level of your job, so check Immigration New Zealand’s conditions before you apply.

Changing employers

An AEWV lets you work only for the accredited employer specified on the visa. You cannot take a second job or be self-employed. To move to a different employer, the visa has to be changed or a new one granted first. Confirm the process with Immigration New Zealand.

Common mistakes

  • A job offer from an employer that is not accredited.
  • A role without an approved job check.
  • Less than 2 years of relevant experience and no qualifying qualification.
  • No English evidence for a skill level 3 to 5 job.
  • Assuming family can be added to the AEWV application.
  • Taking a second job or freelance work on an AEWV.
