UAE Work Visa for Indians: Work Permits, Residence Visas and Costs

To work in the UAE you need two things: a work permit from the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MoHRE) and a residence visa. For most Indian workers the employer applies for both. A visit visa does not let you work.

Overview

Under the UAE Labour Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021), it is illegal to work in the UAE without a valid work permit issued by MoHRE, and employers may not recruit or employ anyone without one.

A work permit and a residence visa are different documents. The work permit authorises the job with a specific establishment. The residence visa, issued by the immigration authorities (ICP, or GDRFA in Dubai), lets you live in the country. Most employees hold both, tied to the same employer.

A smaller group of people can sponsor themselves: skilled employees on a Green visa and holders of a Golden visa. They still need a work permit or contract to take a job, but their residence does not depend on one employer.

MoHRE lists 13 work permit types in all. Others cover transfers between employers, residents on family sponsorship, juveniles, students, trainees and private tutors.

Eligibility

Standard work visa (MoHRE work permit)

  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • The job must match the business activity of the employer’s establishment.
  • The employer’s trade licence must be valid and free of violations.
  • For specialised professions, you must meet the licensing conditions for that profession.
  • The application must be made by someone legally authorised to represent the company.

Green visa for skilled employees

  • A valid work contract and work permit in the UAE.
  • A job at occupational level 1, 2 or 3 in the MoHRE classification.
  • At least a bachelor’s degree or equivalent.
  • A salary of at least AED 15,000 a month.

Golden visa

  • Eligibility depends on the sub-category, such as doctors, scientists, inventors, executives, athletes and PhD holders.
  • Evidence usually includes recommendation letters, experience or accredited degrees.
  • The federal authority provides an online eligibility checker.

Job offer and sponsorship

For the standard route you need a job offer first, because only an establishment registered with MoHRE can apply for the permit. The offer and the employment contract use MoHRE’s standard forms.

Exceptions are the self-sponsored routes. Green visa holders and Golden visa holders are not sponsored by an employer for residence, and freelance permit holders work without an employment contract.

Who does what

Work visa applications fail most often when it is unclear who is responsible for which step. This is how the duties are split.

What the employer does

  • Hold a valid trade licence and stay compliant with the Wages Protection System.
  • Apply for the work permit and the residence visa.
  • Pay the work permit fee, which depends on the company’s MoHRE classification.
  • Renew the permit on time and settle any fines for late issue or renewal.
  • Confirm you have received your dues when a permit is cancelled.

What you do

  • Provide a valid passport, photographs and your qualification documents.
  • Complete the medical fitness test and biometrics for the Emirates ID after arrival.
  • Sign the offer letter and employment contract.
  • Do not start work until the work permit has been issued.
  • For ECR passports: get emigration clearance in India before departure.

Documents

Common documents

  • Valid passport
  • Passport photographs
  • Signed job offer and employment contract on the MoHRE forms
  • Educational or professional certificates required for the role, attested where the authorities ask for it
  • Medical fitness result and Emirates ID biometrics, completed in the UAE
  • For ECR passport holders: emigration clearance obtained through the eMigrate system before departure

How to apply

1

Receive and sign a job offer from a UAE employer registered with MoHRE.

2

The employer applies for the work permit, and the entry permit that lets you travel for work.

3

If your passport carries an Emigration Check Required (ECR) endorsement, obtain emigration clearance from the Protector of Emigrants through India’s eMigrate system before you fly. This applies to employment in all 18 ECR countries, which include the UAE.

4

Enter the UAE on the work entry permit, not on a visit visa.

5

Complete the medical fitness test and give biometrics for your Emirates ID.

6

The employer completes the residence visa. The official Work Bundle platform combines these steps: it needs 5 documents and 2 in-person visits.

7

Renew the permit and visa before they expire, usually every two years.

Family and job changes

Dependants

Residents can sponsor family members if they meet the conditions set by the immigration authorities. Green and Golden visa holders can sponsor a spouse and children under their own residence.

We did not confirm the current salary or housing conditions for family sponsorship from an official page, so check ICP or GDRFA before you apply.

Changing employers

Your standard work permit is tied to one establishment. To move, the new employer applies for a work permit to transfer you; this permit is also valid for two years.

Do not start with the new employer until the transfer permit is issued. When a permit is cancelled, the company must confirm that you have received all your entitlements.

Common mistakes

  • Travelling on a visit or tourist visa and starting work before a work permit is issued.
  • Accepting an offer from a company that is not registered with MoHRE or has a suspended file.
  • Signing a contract with terms that differ from the job offer.
  • Paying an employer or agent for the work permit: the fee is charged to the establishment.
  • Leaving India without emigration clearance when your passport has an ECR endorsement.
  • Letting the permit or residence expire, which leads to fines.
