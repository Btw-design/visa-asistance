What is an Angola Visa?

Angola offers a convenient eVisa system for Indian citizens, making it easier to visit this southwestern African nation known for its natural beauty, diamonds, and oil wealth.

2026 Updates at a Glance: See the Updates table below for all 2026 changes.

Angola has streamlined its visa process with a fully digital eVisa system for Indian citizens. Luanda, the capital, is connected via Dubai, Addis Ababa, and Lisbon.

Indian business travelers frequently visit Angola for opportunities in oil & gas, diamonds, construction, and agriculture. The country also offers Kalandula Falls and Kissama National Park.

Do Indians Need a Visa for Angola?

Yes, Indian citizens need a visa for Angola. An eVisa is available for tourism and business purposes. Visa on arrival is not available.

Best Time to Visit Angola

Angola has a tropical climate with distinct wet and dry seasons. Planning your visit around the weather is important:

  • Dry Season (May to October): The best time to visit, especially for wildlife viewing in Kissama National Park and Iona National Park. Temperatures range from 20-30C with low humidity. Roads are more accessible.
  • Wet Season (November to April): Heavy rainfall, particularly from January to March. Some roads become impassable. However, the landscape is lush and green, and hotel rates are lower. Temperatures remain warm at 25-35C.
  • Coastal vs Inland: Luanda and the coast are humid year-round. The central highlands (Huambo, Lubango) are cooler at 15-25C. The south is drier and more desert-like.
  • Special Events: The Luanda International Jazz Festival (July) and Carnival in Luanda (February) are major cultural events worth planning around.

Common Reasons for Angola Visa Rejection

Angola has one of the strictest visa regimes in Africa. Understanding rejection causes is crucial:

  • Missing Pre-Approval Letter: Angola requires a visa pre-approval letter from the Angolan Immigration Service, which must be obtained before application submission.
  • Incomplete Invitation Letter: The invitation from an Angolan entity must be notarized and approved by the Ministry of Interior. Letters from individual citizens are not accepted.
  • Yellow Fever Certificate Missing: A valid yellow fever vaccination certificate is mandatory for all travelers entering Angola. The vaccination must be administered at least 10 days before travel.
  • Bank Statement Shortfalls: You must show a minimum balance of $500 USD equivalent per day of stay. Statements must be stamped by the bank and cover 6 months.
  • Passport Validity Issues: Passport must have at least 6 months validity and 3 blank pages. Damaged or worn passports are frequently rejected.

Angola visa processing typically takes 10-15 working days. The eVisa system has recently been introduced for tourism, simplifying the process for citizens of over 100 countries.

Travel Tips for Angola

Navigate Angola confidently with these practical tips:

  • Currency: Angolan Kwanza (AOA) is the local currency. The economy is largely cash-based. US Dollars are widely accepted but ensure they are in perfect condition. ATMs are available in Luanda but unreliable elsewhere.
  • Transportation: Luanda's Quatro de Fevereiro Airport (LAD) is the main international gateway. Domestic flights connect to Benguela, Lubango, and Saurimo. Taxis in Luanda are affordable but negotiate fares in advance.
  • Language: Portuguese is the official language. English is not widely spoken outside hotels and business centers in Luanda. Learning basic Portuguese phrases is highly recommended.
  • Safety: Exercise caution in Luanda, particularly after dark. Avoid displaying valuables. Stick to registered taxis rather than public transport. Keep copies of your passport and visa at all times.
  • Health: Malaria is prevalent. Take prophylactic medication and use mosquito repellent. Yellow fever vaccination is mandatory. Typhoid and Hepatitis A vaccinations are recommended.
  • Cuisine: Try Muamba de Galinha (chicken stew), Calulu (fish with vegetables), and fresh tropical fruits. The seafood in Luanda is excellent. Drink bottled water only.
