Do Indians need a visa for Estonia?

Estonia is in the Schengen Area, so Indian passport holders need a Schengen visa. Apply to Estonia when it is your main destination, through a VFS Global centre in India. Applications are decided within 15 days.

Estonia visa options for Indians

OptionWho it is forStay and validityOfficial fee
Schengen short-stay visaTourism, business and family visitsUp to 90 days in any 180-day period€90, the standard Schengen fee

Estonia visa fees

OptionOfficial fee
Schengen short-stay visa€90, the standard Schengen fee

The Embassy of Estonia in New Delhi publishes the visa fee on its visa fee page; €90 is the standard Schengen fee for adults. VFS Global also collects a service fee of €22, charged in Indian rupees; children under 6 do not pay it.

Documents required

Official sources do not give a full list for every case; these are the documents applicants are normally asked for. Confirm the exact list when you apply.

  • Completed Schengen visa application form with photograph
  • Passport valid for at least three months after you plan to leave the Schengen Area, issued within the previous 10 years
  • Travel medical insurance covering the Schengen Area
  • Flight reservation and proof of accommodation
  • Proof of funds and of your employment or studies
  • Supporting documents for the purpose of your trip

How to apply

1

Apply to Estonia only if it is your main destination in the Schengen Area.

2

Submit your application at a VFS Global visa centre in India. VFS is the only accredited agency for Estonian visa applications.

3

Pay the visa fee and the VFS service fee, and give your biometrics.

4

Apply no earlier than six months and no later than 15 days before your trip.

Processing time

Within 15 days of a complete application being lodged; this can be extended to a maximum of 45 calendar days in individual cases.
