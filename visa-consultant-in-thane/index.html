Appointments

Where Thane applicants attend appointments

Embassies do not accept applications in Thane itself. For most countries, Thane residents submit documents and give biometrics at a visa application centre in Mumbai, and many of those centres are in Bandra Kurla Complex (BKC).

For a US visa, both stages are in BKC: fingerprints and photo at the Visa Application Center, then the interview at the US Consulate General Mumbai.

Centres, addresses and opening hours change, and each country uses its own provider. We confirm the correct centre and what to carry when we book your slot.

Plan your appointment day

  • Leave early

    Allow for traffic or the train from Thane to BKC; late arrivals can lose the slot.

  • Carry originals

    Bring your passport, appointment letter and the originals of what you uploaded.

  • Check the rules on bags and phones

    Many centres restrict electronics and large bags.

Being clear with you

What a visa consultant can and cannot do

Visa decisions are made only by embassies and consulates. Your inquiry will be handled by BTW Visa’s visa assistance team.

We do

  • Explain the requirements, fees and timelines for your visa
  • Review your documents and point out gaps
  • Help you complete forms and book appointments

We do not

  • Guarantee approval — no one honestly can
  • Act for any government, embassy, consulate or visa centre
  • Arrange jobs, sponsorship or documents you do not have
