Dubai Visa for Indians in 2026: Who Gets Visa on Arrival

Most Indians still need a visa before flying to Dubai. But if you already hold a visa or residence permit from certain countries, you can get a UAE visa on arrival, and that list grew in 2026.

Who qualifies for visa on arrival

You qualify if you hold an Indian ordinary passport valid for at least 6 months and one of these:

  • A valid US visa, US residence permit or Green Card
  • A valid visa or residence permit from the UK or an EU country
  • A valid visa, residence permit or Green Card from Singapore, Japan, South Korea, Australia, New Zealand or Canada

Accompanying family members can also qualify under the June 2026 update announced by the UAE’s ICP.

Everyone else: get a UAE tourist visa before you fly

If you don’t hold one of those visas, apply for a UAE tourist e-visa in advance. The common routes are:

  • Through your airline. Emirates, flydubai, Air India and others offer visa services when you book with them.
  • Through an authorised travel agent in India.
  • Through official UAE portals such as ICP or GDRFA Dubai.

Tourist visas usually come as 30-day or 60-day options, single or multiple entry. Prices vary by route and visa type, and fees are not refunded if the visa is refused.

Typical documents

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months, with a clear scan of the front and back pages
  • Recent passport-size photo on a white background
  • Confirmed return flight
  • Hotel booking, or the host’s details if staying with family

Tips for a smooth trip

  • Check your other visa’s validity. For visa on arrival, the US, UK, EU or other visa must still be valid when you land.
  • Apply 1 to 2 weeks ahead in peak season (November to March) and around Diwali and New Year.
  • Don’t overstay. Overstay fines add up daily and can affect future UAE visas.
