US Visa Wait Times in India: Is the $750 Fast-Track an Option?

October 13, 20264 min readAjit Mane
  • Introduction
  • The $750 pilot
  • Earlier interview
  • FAQs
  • Sources

As reported on 13 October 2026; check the official US visa scheduling site for the current list of participating posts

First-time US visitor visa (B1/B2) applicants in India often face waits of many months for an interview, with some reports of more than a year. A new paid fast-track exists, but as of the latest reports it is not available at any US post in India.

The $750 expedited appointment pilot

The US State Department is running a temporary pilot from 1 July to 31 December 2026 for B1 (business) and B2 (tourist) visa applicants.

DetailWhat it means
Extra fee$750 per person, on top of the $185 visa application (MRV) fee
What you getAn interview appointment within about 10 business days
What you don’t getFaster processing after the interview, or a guaranteed approval
If refusedThe $750 is not refunded
WhereOnly at selected US embassies and consulates

Is India included? Not yet. As of late August 2026, none of the five US posts in India (New Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata) had been named as participating. More posts may be added before the pilot ends, so check the official scheduling site.

Watch out for scams. The fee is paid only through the official visa system. Anyone in India offering to take $750 in cash to speed up your appointment is not selling a government service.

How to get an earlier interview from India

  1. Check every city, not just yours. You can interview at any of the five US posts in India. Wait times differ a lot between cities, so compare them on the State Department’s wait-times page.
  2. Check for an interview waiver. If you are renewing a B1/B2 visa that expired recently, you may qualify for the “dropbox” route with no interview. Eligibility was narrowed in 2025, so confirm the current rules on the official site.
  3. Keep checking for released slots. New appointments open periodically, often at short notice. Log in regularly once you have paid the MRV fee.
  4. Request an emergency appointment if it is genuine. After booking a regular slot, you can request an expedited appointment for urgent medical, family or business reasons, with supporting documents. There is no extra fee for this.
  5. Apply early. If you are planning a US trip in 2027, pay the fee and book now. The MRV fee receipt stays valid for a year.

Once you have a date, see our US visa interview tips and the US visa guide.
