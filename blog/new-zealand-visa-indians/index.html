New Zealand Tourist Visa for Indians 2026: Step-by-Step Application Guide

Published: August 1, 2026 9 min read By Ajit Mane Updated 2026

New Zealand has rapidly become one of the most popular travel destinations for Indian travellers. From the breathtaking fjords of Milford Sound to the geothermal wonders of Rotorua and the vibrant multicultural city of Auckland, New Zealand offers an unforgettable mix of natural beauty, adventure, and welcoming culture. Every year, thousands of Indians visit New Zealand for tourism, to see family members, or to attend conferences and events — and obtaining the right visitor visa is the first step of that journey.

If you are an Indian citizen planning a trip to New Zealand in 2026, this comprehensive guide walks you through everything you need to know about the New Zealand tourist visa (officially called the Visitor Visa). We cover eligibility requirements, documents needed, fees, processing times, and the complete step-by-step application process so you can apply with confidence.

Key Fact

India is one of the fastest-growing source markets for New Zealand tourism. Visitor visa applications from India are generally processed within 15–30 working days, and success depends largely on how well you demonstrate your financial capacity, ties to India, and genuine purpose of travel.

New Zealand Visitor Visa Overview

The New Zealand Visitor Visa, issued by Immigration New Zealand (INZ), allows you to stay in New Zealand temporarily for tourism, visiting friends and family, or short-term business activities. Indian citizens are required to hold a Visitor Visa before travelling to New Zealand, except for very limited transit situations.

Here are the key types of temporary visitor visas relevant to Indian travellers:

Visitor Visa Types:
  • Standard Visitor Visa — For tourism, sightseeing, and holidays, usually valid for up to 9 months in any 18-month period.
  • Visitor Visa to Visit Family — For Indians with family members (parents, siblings, children) who are New Zealand citizens or residents.
  • Group Visitor Visa — For groups of travellers visiting New Zealand together for tourism.
  • Parent and Grandparent Visitor Visa — A three-year multi-entry visa for parents and grandparents of New Zealand citizens or residents, with a maximum stay of 6 months per visit.
  • Transit Visa — For travellers passing through New Zealand to another destination, with a stay of up to 24 hours.

Most tourist visa holders receive a visitor visa valid for up to 9 months during a single stay. However, the actual validity period granted depends on factors such as your purpose of travel, financial standing, and your ties back home. A visa officer decides how long you may stay and may place conditions on your visa, such as a single entry or multiple entries.

Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for a New Zealand tourist visa as an Indian citizen, you must satisfy Immigration New Zealand that you are a genuine visitor who will comply with the conditions of the visa. The key eligibility requirements include:

  • Genuine intention to visit — You must prove that you genuinely intend to visit New Zealand for tourism, family visits, or limited business and will leave at the end of your stay.
  • Sufficient funds — You must demonstrate you can financially support yourself during your stay, typically NZD $1,000 per month of stay (or NZD $400 per month if accommodation is already paid for), plus funds for an onward or return ticket.
  • Good health — You must be in good health. Some applicants may be asked to undergo a chest X-ray or medical examination, particularly for stays of six months or longer.
  • Good character — You must not have a significant criminal record. Immigration New Zealand will request police certificates if you have been convicted of offences or stayed in certain countries for a cumulative period of 24 months or more.
  • Valid passport — Your passport must be valid for at least three months beyond your intended departure date from New Zealand.
  • Onward travel arrangements — You should have a return or onward air ticket, or demonstrate the funds to purchase one.
Pro Tip: Indians under 18 years of age travelling without a parent or guardian need additional documentation, including written consent from their legal guardians and details of the person caring for them in New Zealand. Plan this paperwork well in advance.

Documents Required

Submitting a complete and well-organised document set is one of the most important factors in getting your New Zealand visitor visa approved. Here is a detailed checklist of the documents required for Indian applicants:

Document Checklist:
  • Valid passport with at least two blank pages, valid for 3 months beyond your planned departure from New Zealand.
  • Completed and signed Visa Application Form (INZ 1017 for general visitor visa).
  • Two recent passport-size photographs meeting New Zealand visa photo specifications.
  • Proof of sufficient funds — bank statements for the last 6 months, fixed deposit certificates, and income tax returns.
  • Proof of employment — employment letter, salary slips for the last 3 months, and leave approval letter.
  • For self-employed applicants — business registration, GST returns, and a letter explaining your business activities.
  • Cover letter explaining the purpose of your visit, planned itinerary, and proof of ties to India.
  • Flight itinerary and hotel bookings for the duration of your stay.
  • Invitation letter from family or friends in New Zealand, along with their visa or residence status, if applicable.
  • Travel insurance covering medical emergencies for your entire stay.
  • Ties to home country — property documents, family details, or ongoing commitments in India.
  • If applicable, an approved chest X-ray or medical certificate from an INZ-approved panel physician.
  • Marriage certificate if travelling with a spouse, and a notarised no-objection letter from guardians for minor travellers.

All documents that are not in English must be translated into English by a certified translator. Translations should include the translator's certification that they are accurate, along with their signature and date. Incomplete or inconsistent documentation is one of the leading reasons for visitor visa refusals, so take time to review everything before submission.

Important: You do not need to submit original documents unless specifically requested by Immigration New Zealand. However, all copies you provide must be high quality, legible, and certified if requested.

How to Apply for NZ Visitor Visa

The New Zealand visitor visa application process for Indian citizens is straightforward and can be completed online through the Immigration New Zealand website. Here are the five key steps:

1

Prepare Your Documents

Gather all the documents listed in the checklist above. Scan each document as a high-quality PDF or JPG and make sure the file sizes meet the upload requirements. Organise your financial evidence, employment proof, itinerary, and cover letter so everything tells one consistent story.

2

Complete the Online Application

Create an account on the Immigration New Zealand online portal and complete the Visitor Visa online form (INZ 1017 digital version). Fill in every field accurately — your passport details, personal history, travel plans, and health and character declarations must match your supporting documents exactly.

3

Upload Documents and Pay the Fee

Upload all scanned documents and pay the visa application fee online using an international credit or debit card. The fee includes the application fee plus the Immigration Levy. Keep the payment receipt and application reference number safe — you will need them to track your application.

4

Attend Biometrics Appointment (If Required)

In most cases, Indian applicants will be asked to provide biometrics (fingerprints and photograph) at a VFS Global application centre in India after submitting the online application. You will receive an appointment notification, and you must attend with your passport, appointment letter, and the necessary documents.

5

Track Your Application and Receive the Decision

After your biometrics are submitted, Immigration New Zealand processes your application. You can track the status online using your application reference. Most straightforward applications from India are decided within 15–30 working days. If approved, you will receive an electronic visa confirmation (e-visa) by email.

Fees are set by Immigration New Zealand and may change with the government's annual fee review. The exact amount payable in INR will vary based on the prevailing exchange rate and any service charges applied by VFS Global, which handles document submission and biometrics for Indian applicants. Always confirm the latest fees on the Immigration New Zealand website before you apply.

Pro Tip: If you are concerned about processing times, apply at least one to two months before your planned travel date. Peak seasons such as December and the New Zealand summer (November–February) can significantly lengthen processing times.

Tips for a Successful Application

Approval for a New Zealand visitor visa depends on your ability to convince the visa officer that you are a genuine visitor who will return to India. These practical tips will strengthen your application:

Application Success Tips:
  • Show a stable and consistent bank balance for at least six months — avoid sudden large deposits right before applying.
  • Write a detailed cover letter outlining your travel plan, itinerary, and clearly explaining what you will do in New Zealand.
  • Provide strong evidence of ties to India, such as stable employment, family responsibilities, property, or business interests.
  • Include previous travel history — prior visas to countries like the UK, Schengen, Australia, or the US demonstrate you have returned home before.
  • Match every detail across documents — names, dates, passport numbers, and addresses must be identical everywhere.
  • If you are visiting family, include their New Zealand visa or residence status and an invitation letter.
  • Purchase travel insurance for the entire duration of your stay, even though it is not strictly mandatory.
  • If you have a previous visa refusal, address it honestly in your cover letter and explain what has changed.
  • Submit high-quality scanned copies that are complete, legible, and within the permitted file size limits.
Important: Never submit false information or fabricated documents. Immigration New Zealand performs strict verification checks, and providing false documents leads to a mandatory refusal plus a ban on applying for New Zealand visas for a significant period.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How long can an Indian tourist stay in New Zealand on a visitor visa?
A: A standard visitor visa generally allows a stay of up to 9 months in any 18-month period for Indian visitors. Some visa conditions, such as multiple entries, may give you more flexibility. For short-stay trips most applicants are granted between one and six months. Check the conditions printed on your visa letter carefully.

Q: Do Indian citizens need a visa for New Zealand?
A: Yes. Indian citizens require a Visitor Visa (or eligible transit visa) to travel to New Zealand for tourism, family visits, or business. There is no visa-free or eTA arrangement for Indian passport holders, so you must apply through Immigration New Zealand before your trip.

Q: What is the processing time for a New Zealand visitor visa from India?
A: Most straightforward visitor visa applications from India are processed within 15–30 working days. Applications during peak travel seasons, or those requiring additional verification such as medical checks or police certificates, can take longer. Check current processing times on the Immigration New Zealand website before applying.

Q: How much bank balance do I need for a New Zealand tourist visa?
A: You should be able to show approximately NZD $1,000 per month of intended stay (NZD $400 per month if accommodation is already paid), plus funds for a return ticket. In practice, demonstrate a stable, consistent bank balance representing at least three to six months of income, ideally with savings comfortably above the minimum requirement.

Q: Can I work in New Zealand on a visitor visa?
A: No. A Visitor Visa strictly prohibits working in New Zealand. You may undertake limited business-related activities such as attending meetings or conferences, but you cannot take up employment or offer services for payment. Working illegally can lead to visa cancellation and removal from New Zealand.
