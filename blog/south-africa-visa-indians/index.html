South Africa Tourist Visa for Indians 2026: Documents, Fees & Processing Time

Published: August 1, 2026 10 min read By Ajit Mane Updated 2026

South Africa is one of the most unique and rewarding travel destinations for Indian tourists. Whether you dream of a safari in Kruger National Park, the iconic views from Table Mountain in Cape Town, the cultural energy of Johannesburg, or the scenic Garden Route, South Africa offers an unforgettable experience that blends wildlife, breathtaking landscapes, and rich history. In 2026, growing flight connectivity and a welcoming travel environment have made it an increasingly popular choice for Indian families, couples, and adventure travellers.

Before you pack your bags, however, you need to understand the South African visa process. Indian passport holders must secure a visitor's visa before travelling, and getting the application right the first time saves you both money and stress. This complete guide covers everything you need to know — visa types, eligibility, required documents, the step-by-step application process, fees, processing times, and expert tips for approval.

Key Fact

South African visitor's visas for Indian nationals permit a stay of up to 90 days for tourism, family visits, and business in most cases. Applications are processed through the Department of Home Affairs (DHA), with submissions and biometrics handled at VFS Global centres across major Indian cities.

South Africa Tourist Visa Overview

South Africa issues a range of visas depending on the purpose of your visit. For travel purposes, Indian applicants typically apply for the following:

  • Visitor's Visa (Tourist): For tourism, holidays, sightseeing, and leisure, normally valid for up to 90 days.
  • Visitor's Visa (Family / Business): For visiting family members residing in South Africa or attending short business meetings, conferences, and trade events.
  • Transit Visa: For passengers transiting through a South African port of entry on their way to another country.

Each visa type has its own documentation requirements, but the core submission process and general eligibility criteria remain similar. Throughout this guide, we focus on the most common type for Indian travellers — the leisure/visitor's visa.

Eligibility Requirements

To be eligible for a South African visitor's visa, you must be able to prove that you are a genuine visitor with sufficient means to support your stay and strong reasons to return to India. The South African authorities assess your application holistically, and the following conditions apply:

  • You hold an Indian passport valid for at least 30 days beyond your intended departure from South Africa.
  • Your passport contains at least two completely blank pages for visa endorsement.
  • You intend to visit for tourism, family visits, or short business engagements within the permitted duration.
  • You can demonstrate sufficient financial resources to cover accommodation, transport, and daily expenses for the entire stay.
  • You have clear ties to India — employment, business, family, or property — that ensure your return.
  • You have not been refused entry or deported from South Africa or any other country in the past.

Applicants under the age of 18 have additional requirements, including unconditional consent from both parents or legal guardians, either in person at submission or through a notarised parental consent letter.

Documents Required

Submitting a complete and consistent document file is the key to a smooth South African visa application. Below is the standard checklist for Indian applicants:

Document Checklist:
  • A valid Indian passport with a minimum of 30 days validity beyond your stay and at least two blank pages.
  • A fully completed South African visa application form (BI-84), signed and dated.
  • Two recent passport-sized photographs taken against a plain background as per DHA specifications.
  • Proof of accommodation — confirmed hotel bookings or a letter of invitation from your host in South Africa.
  • A return flight itinerary and proof of onward travel plans.
  • Bank statements for the last three to six months showing sufficient funds for your stay.
  • Proof of employment (employer letter, salary slips) or business registration documents if self-employed.
  • Income tax returns or other evidence of financial standing in India.
  • A cover letter outlining your purpose of visit, itinerary, and ties to India.
  • A Yellow Fever vaccination certificate if you are arriving from (or transiting through) a yellow fever endemic country.

How to Apply for South Africa Tourist Visa

The application process is managed by VFS Global on behalf of the South African Department of Home Affairs. Here are the five key steps involved:

1

Prepare Your Documents

Gather every document from the checklist above and verify that names, dates, passport numbers, and addresses match exactly across all documents. Arrange clear colour copies of your originals and keep them in a logical order for ease of verification.

2

Book an Appointment at VFS Global

Schedule an appointment at the nearest VFS Global South Africa visa application centre in India (available in cities such as New Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Pune, and others). Booking early is strongly advised, especially during peak holiday seasons when slots fill quickly.

3

Submit Your Application and Biometrics

Attend your appointment in person with all required documents, your completed application form, and the applicable fees. Your fingerprints and photograph will be captured, and you will sign your declaration before the application is accepted.

4

Pay the Visa and Service Fees

Pay the non-refundable visa application fee plus the VFS Global service charge at the time of submission using the accepted payment methods. Keep your payment receipt and application tracking number safe — you will need them to track your application.

5

Track Your Application and Collect Your Passport

Once submitted, your application is processed by the South African Department of Home Affairs. You can track progress online using your reference number. When a decision is made, your passport is returned either for collection at the centre or by courier to your address.

Fees and Processing Time

The total cost of a South African visitor's visa includes a non-refundable visa application fee plus VFS Global service charges. The table below gives a general overview:

Fee / StepDetails
Visa application feeApprox. ZAR 800 (about ₹3,700) per applicant, subject to exchange rate fluctuations
VFS Global service chargeAs per the current published fee schedule at the time of submission
BiometricsCompleted free of charge at the VFS Global centre during your appointment
Processing timeTypically 5–15 working days for straightforward applications; may be longer during peak periods or if additional checks are needed

Fees are payable at the time of submission and are not refundable, even if your application is refused. Always confirm the latest fee schedule and processing timelines with the official DHA or VFS Global before you apply.

Tips for a Successful Application

A well-prepared application dramatically reduces the risk of refusal or delay. Here is practical advice from our visa consultants:

Pro Tip: Provide genuine, verifiable financial documents. The DHA closely checks bank statements for sudden large deposits, inconsistent income, or insufficient balances. Stable savings over several months tell a far more convincing story than a one-off transfer before your application date.
Success Checklist:
  • Apply 6–8 weeks before your intended travel date to allow plenty of buffer for processing.
  • Ensure your passport has blank pages and more than 30 days of validity beyond your return date.
  • Submit a clear, well-written cover letter with your full itinerary and reasons to return to India.
  • Include proof of employment or business ownership along with salary slips and ITR filings.
  • Check whether you need a Yellow Fever certificate if your journey routes through an endemic country.
  • Do not book non-refundable flights or hotels until your visa has been approved.
  • Triple-check your application form for errors — mistakes in names or passport details are a common cause of delays.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do Indian citizens need a visa for South Africa?
A: Yes. Indian passport holders are required to obtain a visitor's visa before travelling to South Africa for tourism, family visits, or short business trips. There is currently no visa-free arrangement for Indian nationals.

Q: Is South Africa issuing e-visas for Indian travellers?
A: South Africa has been piloting electronic visas (eVisa) through its official visa services portal for select nationalities, including India in eligible categories. Availability can change frequently, so you should verify the current eVisa status with the Department of Home Affairs or VFS Global before deciding how to apply.

Q: How long can I stay in South Africa on a tourist visa?
A: The standard South African visitor's visa issued to Indian tourists permits a stay of up to 90 days. The exact duration granted is at the discretion of the immigration officer reviewing your application.

Q: How long does it take to process a South Africa visitor's visa from India?
A: Processing typically takes between 5 and 15 working days for complete, straightforward applications. During peak seasons or if additional verification is required, processing can take longer, so it is best to apply well in advance of your travel date.

Q: What is the visa fee for a South Africa tourist visa from India?
A: The South African visa application fee is approximately ZAR 800 (about ₹3,700) per applicant, plus the VFS Global service charge. Fees are non-refundable and subject to change, so always confirm the current schedule before applying.
