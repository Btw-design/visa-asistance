UAE Visit Visa for Indians 2026: Free 30-Day Visa, New Rules & How to Apply

Published: August 1, 2026 10 min read By Ajit Mane Updated 2026

The United Arab Emirates continues to be one of the friendliest destinations for Indian travellers, and 2026 brings fresh clarity to its visa policies. From the free 30-day visa on arrival for eligible Indian passport holders to longer multi-entry options, the Emirates have streamlined the way tourists from India plan their trips to Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, and beyond.

However, with new digital platforms, updated document requirements, and several policy changes announced by the UAE authorities, it is easy to get confused about which visa you actually need. This complete guide explains whether Indians need a visa to visit the UAE in 2026, who qualifies for the free 30-day entry, the documents required, application steps, fees, and the latest rule changes you must know.

Key Fact

Indian passport holders generally receive a free 30-day visa on arrival when entering the UAE, provided their passport is valid for at least six months and they meet the applicable conditions. The stay can be extended for a further 30 days by paying a service fee, subject to the approval of UAE immigration authorities.

Do Indians Need a Visa to Visit UAE?

The short answer is: it depends on your nationality document, the emirate you are flying into, and your travel purpose. For standard Indian passports used for tourism, many travellers do not need to apply for a visa in advance because they can obtain a visa on arrival. In other cases, a sponsored visit visa must be arranged before you fly.

Here are the main visa options available to Indian citizens in 2026:

  • Free 30-day visa on arrival — automatic entry for eligible Indian passport holders arriving by air for tourism.
  • 90-day visa on arrival — available to certain travellers holding a US visa, EU residence permit, or other qualifying visas issued by select countries.
  • Sponsored visit visa — a 30, 60, or 90-day visa arranged through an airline, hotel, company, or UAE-based sponsor before travel.
  • 5-year multiple-entry tourist visa — a longer-term option for frequent visitors.
  • Remote work visa — for travellers who want to stay in the UAE while working for an overseas employer.

Free 30-Day Visa on Arrival

The free 30-day visa on arrival is the most popular option for Indian tourists visiting the UAE. Under this arrangement, eligible Indian passport holders are granted a visa stamped at the airport on arrival, with no advance application or fee required.

The key eligibility conditions to keep in mind are:

  • You must hold an ordinary Indian passport valid for at least 6 months from your date of entry.
  • You must be arriving for tourism or a short family/business visit — not for employment.
  • Your entry must be through a UAE international airport (land border crossings follow the same scheme in most cases, but always confirm with authorities).
  • Your arrival and stay remain subject to the final discretion of UAE immigration officers.
  • The visa is valid for 30 days and can be extended for a further 30 days once, for a fee.
Pro Tip: Always keep a return ticket, hotel booking, and proof of sufficient funds ready. While they are not always requested, immigration officers can ask for them, and having them at hand makes your entry faster and smoother.

Documents Required for UAE Visit Visa

Whether you are getting a visa on arrival or applying for a sponsored visit visa, keeping your documents ready saves a lot of time. Here is the complete checklist:

  • Valid Indian passport with at least 6 months of validity remaining.
  • Recent passport-size photograph (required for online visit visa applications).
  • Confirmed return flight ticket or onward travel itinerary.
  • Hotel booking confirmation or a letter of accommodation from your sponsor.
  • Proof of sufficient funds, such as the latest bank statement.
  • Copies of your sponsor's passport or Emirates ID if applying through a sponsor.
  • Travel insurance covering medical emergencies (highly recommended).
  • For business visitors — an invitation letter from the UAE company you are visiting.
Important: If you apply for a visit visa through a sponsor or travel agent, make sure your passport data matches your application exactly. Even minor mismatches can delay approval or result in a rejection that affects your future applications.

How to Apply for UAE Visit Visa

Depending on your eligibility, there are two main routes: entry on arrival or an online visit visa application. Follow these steps for a smooth application:

1

Confirm Your Eligibility

Check whether you qualify for the free 30-day visa on arrival. If you do, you can simply fly and complete the process at immigration. If not, you will need a pre-arranged sponsored visit visa.

2

Choose the Right Visa Type and Channel

For a sponsored visit visa, apply through your airline (for an airline-sponsored visa), hotel, UAE-based company, or an authorised visa service. Applications for Dubai are handled through the GDRFA portal, while other emirates use the ICP portal.

3

Submit Documents and Pay the Fee

Fill in the application, upload your passport copy and photograph, and pay the applicable visa fee. Keep the application reference number for tracking.

4

Receive Your E-Visa

Once approved, the UAE e-visa is emailed to you as a PDF. Check the validity dates, entry status (single or multiple entry), and the grace period before you travel.

5

Print a Copy for Your Journey

Carry a printed copy of your e-visa along with your passport. While the visa is linked electronically, a hard copy speeds up check-in and immigration procedures.

6

Enter the UAE and Respect the Stay Limit

Upon arrival, ensure you do not exceed your authorised stay. If you wish to extend, do so through the ICP or GDRFA portal before your visa expires to avoid overstay fines.

New Rules for 2026

The UAE regularly updates its visa policies, and 2026 includes several changes that Indian travellers should be aware of:

  • Free 30-day visa on arrival continues for eligible Indian passport holders, with online extension facilities available through the ICP and GDRFA portals.
  • Expanded 5-year multiple-entry tourist visa making repeated visits to the UAE more convenient for frequent travellers from India.
  • Unified digital application platforms — most visit and tourist visa applications are now handled online through the ICP (other emirates) and GDRFA (Dubai) portals, reducing paperwork and in-person visits.
  • Simplified documents for repeat travellers — Indians with a clean UAE travel history may face lighter document requirements on subsequent applications.
  • Higher flexibility on 90-day entries for travellers holding valid US visas, Schengen visas, or residence permits from select countries.
  • Revised overstay guidelines — overstay fines and exit procedures continue to be enforced strictly, so always extend or exit before your visa expires.
Important: UAE visa policies change frequently, and some new rules are introduced with limited notice. Always confirm the latest requirements with the official ICP or GDRFA portals and your travel provider before making non-refundable bookings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do Indians need a visa to visit Dubai in 2026?
A: Many Indian passport holders do not need a pre-arranged visa and can enter with a free 30-day visa on arrival, provided their passport is valid for at least six months and they meet the entry conditions. Others, such as those travelling without a sponsor, may need a sponsored visit visa arranged in advance.

Q: Can I extend my 30-day visa on arrival in the UAE?
A: Yes. The free 30-day visa on arrival can be extended once for an additional 30 days by paying a service fee. The extension must be requested through the ICP or GDRFA portal before your current visa expires.

Q: How much does a UAE visit visa cost in 2026?
A: The 30-day visa on arrival is free. A sponsored 30-day visit visa typically costs between AED 300 and 500, while longer 60 and 90-day visas cost more. The 5-year multiple-entry tourist visa costs about AED 2,500 plus a refundable deposit. Fees vary by sponsor and emirate.

Q: How long does a UAE visit visa take to process?
A: Standard visit visa applications are usually processed within 2–5 working days. Urgent processing is available for an additional fee. The 30-day visa on arrival is issued instantly at the airport when you meet the conditions.

Q: What happens if I overstay my UAE visit visa?
A: Overstaying results in penalties that are calculated per day, and overstay fees must be paid before you leave the country or before your visa is renewed. If you know you will exceed your stay, apply for an extension well before the expiry date to avoid fines.
