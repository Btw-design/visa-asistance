Thailand Visa-Free for Indians: The New 30-Day Rule Explained

October 13, 20264 min readAjit Mane
  • Introduction
  • What changed
  • What to carry
  • FAQs
  • Sources

Rules as reported on 13 October 2026; confirm with the Royal Thai Embassy before you travel

Good news first: Thailand is still visa-free for Indian tourists. What changed is the length of stay. Since 15 September 2026, Indian passport holders get 30 days visa-free, down from 60.

What changed, and when

Thailand’s year of mixed headlines confused many travellers. Here is how it played out:

DateWhat happened
15 Sep 2026New rules in force: Indians get a 30-day visa exemption for tourism
31 Aug 2026Rules published in Thailand’s Royal Gazette
14 Jul 2026Cabinet drops the VOA plan and puts India on the 30-day exemption list
19 May 2026Cabinet scraps the 60-day scheme; India initially slated for Visa on Arrival (15 days, THB 2,000)
Jul 2024Indians added to the 60-day visa-exemption scheme

If you entered Thailand before 15 September on the old 60-day exemption, your stay was not cut short. The 30-day limit applies to arrivals from that date on (TAT News).

What you need to enter Thailand

Visa-free does not mean document-free. Carry these for a smooth arrival:

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months from arrival
  • Return or onward ticket within 30 days
  • Hotel bookings for your stay
  • Proof of funds (cash, card or bank statement), as immigration can ask
  • Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC), filled online within 72 hours before you land; it is mandatory for every visitor

Staying longer than 30 days? Apply for a tourist e-visa in advance at thaievisa.go.th, or ask Thai immigration about a one-time extension once you are in the country.

Crossing by land? Visitors using the 30-day exemption can generally enter through land-border checkpoints no more than twice per calendar year. Air arrivals are not capped this way.

Travelling for work? The exemption is for tourism. Business meetings, conferences or a job offer need the right visa category.
