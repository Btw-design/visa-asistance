Visa facts checked against the Embassy of Japan in India on 13 October 2026

Japan has offered an eVISA to tourists from India since 1 April 2024. You still apply through VFS Global, but you get your visa as an electronic notice instead of a sticker in your passport.

Who can use the eVISA

DetailJapan eVISA from India
Visa typeSingle-entry short-term tourism only
StayUp to 90 days
Who can applyIndian nationals and foreign nationals living in India
Where to applyJapan Visa Application Centres run by VFS Global
Fee₹500 for Indian nationals (from 1 July 2026), plus VFS service fee
When to applyUp to 3 months before travel

Business trips, family visits on a multiple-entry visa and longer stays still need a traditional sticker visa. For business trips, see our guide to the Japan business visa.

How to apply

  1. Prepare your documents (checklist below).
  2. Submit at your VFS Japan centre. Use the centre covering the state where you live. No prior appointment is needed.
  3. Pay the fee of ₹500 plus the VFS service charge.
  4. Receive your visa issuance notice electronically once approved.

Typical documents

  • Passport with at least two blank pages
  • Completed and signed visa application form with a recent photo
  • Flight booking and day-by-day itinerary in Japan
  • Hotel bookings
  • Bank statement for the last 6 months and latest income tax return
  • Employment letter, or business registration if self-employed

For photo rules, see our visa photo requirements tool.

Showing your eVISA at the airport

This is where travellers get caught out. You must show the visa issuance notice live on a mobile device, and you need internet access to load it.

A PDF, photo, screenshot or printout is not accepted. Make sure you have roaming, a Japan eSIM or airport Wi-Fi before you reach immigration.

Planning for cherry-blossom season

Sakura season usually runs from late March to early April. Applications open 3 months before travel, so for an early April trip apply from early January. Book flights and hotels early, as prices rise sharply in that window.
