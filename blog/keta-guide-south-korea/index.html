K-ETA for Indians 2026: Why It Does Not Apply and What You Need Instead

Published: August 1, 2026 9 min read By Ajit Mane Updated 7 October 2026

Visa facts checked against official sources on 7 October 2026.

Indian passport holders cannot use K-ETA. The Korea Electronic Travel Authorization is only for nationals of countries that may enter South Korea without a visa, and India is not one of them. Indian travellers need a visa before they fly.

An earlier version of this article said Indians could travel on a K-ETA. That was wrong, and this page has been corrected against the official sources listed at the end.

Key fact

If you hold an Indian passport, a K-ETA does not let you board a flight to or enter South Korea. Apply for a visa instead; for tourism that is the short-term tourist visa (C-3-9), which costs US$40 and takes about 2 weeks.

What K-ETA is, and who it is for

K-ETA is an online travel authorisation run by the Korean government. It is a pre-arrival check for people who already have visa-free access to South Korea; the Korean foreign ministry currently lists nationals of 112 countries as able to apply. It is not a visa and it does not create visa-free access for anyone else.

India is not on that list. For Indian citizens, the entry document for a holiday, a family visit or a business trip is a visa issued by a Korean mission.

What Indians need instead: the C-3-9 tourist visa

ItemDetails
Visa typeShort-term tourist visa (C-3-9), single entry
Official feeUS$40 for Indian nationals, paid in rupees; the visa application centre adds a service charge
Processing timeAbout 2 weeks
Where to applyThe Korea visa application centre that covers your state

Documents

The Korean Embassy lists these for a tourist visa:

  • Passport
  • Visa application form
  • Photo
  • Flight booking
  • Hotel booking
  • Bank statement

How to apply

  1. Fill in the visa application form and attach a photo.
  2. Collect your flight booking, hotel booking and bank statements.
  3. Submit at the Korea visa application centre that covers your state and pay the fee.
  4. Collect your passport after about 2 weeks. Apply well before your travel date.

Avoid paying for a K-ETA you cannot use

  • Unofficial websites sell K-ETA applications to anyone who pays. An approval obtained that way does not replace a visa for an Indian passport holder.
  • The only official K-ETA site is k-eta.go.kr. The only official source for visa rules in India is the Embassy of the Republic of Korea.
  • Airlines check entry documents at check-in. Without a visa you can be refused boarding.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can Indian citizens apply for K-ETA?
A: No. K-ETA is only for nationals of countries that can enter South Korea without a visa. India is not on that list, so Indian passport holders need a visa.

Q: What visa do Indian tourists need for South Korea?
A: The short-term tourist visa (C-3-9). The Korean Embassy lists a fee of US$40 for Indian nationals and processing of about 2 weeks.

Q: Is there a visa on arrival in South Korea for Indians?
A: We found no visa on arrival for Indian tourists on the official Korean pages we checked. Get your visa before you travel.

Q: I already paid a website for a K-ETA. What now?
A: A K-ETA does not replace a visa for an Indian passport. Apply for the visa through the Korea visa application centre, and ask the website you paid about a refund.
